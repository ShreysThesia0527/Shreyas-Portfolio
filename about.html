ABOUT ME

Bridging compliance and technical execution.

I'm an Information Security and Compliance Associate at JJIT Fintech Pvt Ltd. My work spans ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO 9001 compliance, internal audit, banking-client audit support, risk management, and day-to-day IT security activities.

I help maintain ISMS documentation and risk records, assess control gaps, validate audit evidence, and coordinate remediation with responsible teams. Alongside GRC work, I work with security controls covering endpoint protection, PAM, DLP, network security, vulnerability management, and SOC alert handling.

JJIT's fintech services support more than 50 banks. My work contributes to security and compliance activities relevant to this environment; it does not mean I personally own every bank's audit or security program.

I enjoy improving how security work is performed: making asset records easier to track, structuring compliance evidence, documenting patch and hardening activities, and exploring practical ways to strengthen monitoring.
